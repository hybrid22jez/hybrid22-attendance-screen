How each number is worked out. Data from Momence every morning (Mindbody from 4 Jan). Members: people with an active paid membership (direct debit or upfront); trials, class packs, Recovery Lounge and complimentary passes don't count, and frozen members (anyone currently frozen, however long ago they froze and with or without an end date) are shown apart. Leads: people who start a Free Week Trial or 2 Weeks Free. Converted: those leads from the last 30 days who now hold a paid membership, with 12 month shown separately. Churn: cancellations this month ÷ members at the start of the month; a member who leaves while frozen counts as a cancel on the day they leave. Attendance: classes attended per member per week over the last 7 days. CAC: monthly acquisition spend (advertising, other marketing incl. Zane and Alana's retainer, Tay's sales wages, commissions) ÷ new members in the last 30 days. Jez's time and rent are general business costs, not CAC. Trend lines start on the first day of data and fill in week by week.
